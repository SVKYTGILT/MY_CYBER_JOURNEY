# 🛡️ Cyber-Tracker

### What if your cybersecurity roadmap could actually show what you know? 👀

Cybersecurity is HUGE.

There are so many things to learn — networking, Linux, web security, cloud, operating systems, SOC, ethical hacking, certifications, projects... the list just keeps going. 😭

And when I started thinking about my own journey, I had one problem:

**Learning is one thing. Knowing whether I'm actually progressing is another.**

That's why I started building **Cyber-Tracker**. 🚀

It's not just a roadmap where I tick a box and say *"yep, learned this."*

The idea is to make my entire cybersecurity journey **visible, trackable, and testable.**

---

## 🗺️ Follow the Roadmap

Cyber-Tracker gives the journey some structure.

Instead of wondering:

> *"Okay... what am I supposed to learn next?"* 🤔

I can follow the roadmap step by step.

Complete topics.

Move through milestones.

Set target dates.

Track progress.

And see what's coming next.

If I fall behind, that's visible too.

Because let's be honest...

**plans don't always go according to plan.** 😂

---

## 📊 See Your Actual Progress

The dashboard is basically the place where everything comes together.

I can see what I've completed, what's still pending, study activity, certifications, streaks, and overall roadmap progress.

No more opening five different places just to figure out:

**"How far have I actually come?"** 😭

Open the dashboard.

Check.

Keep moving.

---

# 🧠 Learn → Test → Improve

This is one of the parts I'm most excited about.

A checkbox can tell me that I *finished* a topic.

But...

### **Did I actually understand it? 👀**

That's where the new **In-Built Test Section** comes in. 🧪🔥

After learning a topic, I can test myself right inside Cyber-Tracker.

Learn something.

Take the test.

See the result.

Find the weak areas.

Go back and study.

Try again.

That changes the meaning of progress.

It's no longer just:

**Networking ✅**

It can become:

**Networking ✅ | Assessment: 86% 🎯**

The goal isn't to collect checkmarks.

### **The goal is to earn them.** 🔥

---

## 📝 Your Notes, Your Knowledge

While learning cybersecurity, I constantly come across things I don't want to forget.

Commands.

Concepts.

Explanations.

Important points.

Random things that suddenly make sense after struggling with them for two hours. 💀

So Cyber-Tracker has a dedicated notes section too.

Over time, those notes can become my own personal cybersecurity knowledge base.

---

## 📅 What Happened This Week?

Some weeks are insanely productive.

Some weeks...

**yeah, let's not talk about those. 💀**

That's why there's a weekly report section.

I can look back and see what I actually completed instead of relying on memory.

What did I finish?

What did I miss?

Where did I slow down?

What should I focus on next?

The idea isn't to make every week perfect.

It's to make the journey honest.

---

## ⏳ When Things Get Delayed...

College happens.

Exams happen.

Life happens.

Sometimes a topic that was supposed to take one week takes three.

Cyber-Tracker keeps track of those delays instead of pretending they never happened.

Set a target date.

Track the milestone.

See the delay.

Adjust the plan.

Keep going. 🚀

---

## 🏆 Certifications

Certifications are another part of the journey.

I can keep track of what I'm planning to take, what I'm working toward, and what I've already completed.

So certifications aren't just floating around separately from the roadmap.

They're part of the bigger picture.

---

## 🤖 Stuck on Something?

That's where the **AI Mentor** comes in.

Sometimes you don't need another 40-minute video.

You just need someone to explain one confusing concept differently. 😭

The AI Mentor is there for that.

Ask.

Explore.

Understand.

Then get back to learning.

It's not here to do the journey for me.

**It's here to help me keep moving.** ⚡

---

## 🎨 And No... It's Not Another Black Hacker Dashboard 😂

I wanted Cyber-Tracker to feel different.

No endless black screens.

No green terminal text everywhere.

No fake "hacker movie" interface.

Instead, I'm going for a more modern, visual, anime-inspired experience. ✨

Something that actually makes me want to open the website and continue learning.

Because cybersecurity can be complicated enough.

The interface doesn't need to be. 😭

---

## 🔐 Everything Stays Connected

Authentication and data persistence are handled with Supabase.

So the important stuff stays connected to the account:

🗺️ Roadmap progress
📝 Notes
🏆 Certifications
📊 Study activity
🧪 Test results
📅 Learning progress

Close the browser.

Come back later.

**Pick up where you left off.**
